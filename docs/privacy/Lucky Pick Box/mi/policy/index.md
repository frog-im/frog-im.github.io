# Pānui Whakawhiti Raraunga ā-Ao

Rā tīmata: 2026-06-12
Whakahoutanga whakamutunga: 2026-06-15

Kāore a Lucky Pick Box e tuku i ngā tāurunga kēmu, hua kēmu, whakaahua kua tīpakohia rānei ki tētahi tūmau e whakahaerehia ana e te kaiwhakawhanake. Ina whakamahia ngā āheinga pānui, whakaaetanga rānei, ka tukatuka pea a Google me ngā kaiwhakatukatuka pātahi i ētahi mōhiohio i waho i tō whenua, rohe rānei.

1. Ngā kaiwhiwhi me ngā whenua

Ka uru pea ki ngā kaiwhiwhi ko Google LLC, ngā hinonga pātahi o Google, me ngā kaiwhakatukatuka e whakamahia ana mō te pānuitanga, whakahaere whakaaetanga, haumaru, ine me te whakahaere ratonga. Ka puta pea te tukatuka i te United States me ētahi atu whenua e pupuri hanganga ana a Google, āna kaiwhakatukatuka rānei.

2. Ngā mōhiohio ka whakawhitia pea

Ka uru pea ko ngā tautuhi pānui, tautuhi instance taupānga, wāhitau IP, tauwāhi tata, mōhiohio pūrere me te whatunga, putanga taupānga, pūnaha whakahaere, reo, kitenga pānui, pāwhiri me ngā pāhekoheko, tātaritanga me ngā tautuhinga whakaaetanga, tūmataitinga pānui rānei. Kāore ngā tāurunga kēmu, tapanga kaitākaro, hua kēmu me te whakaahua kua tīpakohia ake e tukuna e te kaiwhakawhanake ki ngā ratonga pānui a Google.

3. Wā, tikanga me te kaupapa

Ka puta pea te whakawhiti mā ngā whakawhitinga ipurangi kua whakamunatia ina tono pānui te taupānga, ina whakahou i te tūnga whakaaetanga, ina whakatuwhera rānei i ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga. Ko ngā kaupapa ko te tuku pānui, pānui kāore i whakawhaiarotia, here auau, aukati tinihanga, whakahaere whakaaetanga, ine, haumaru ratonga me te ū ki te ture.

4. Pupuri me ngā kōwhiringa

Ka pupuri a Google me āna kaiwhakatukatuka i ngā mōhiohio i raro i ā rātou kaupapahere tūmataitinga, herenga kirimana me te ture e hāngai ana. Ina wātea, ka taea e koe te kōwhiri pānui kāore i whakawhaiarotia, restricted data processing mō ngā ture tūmataitinga ā-kāwanatanga o Amerika e hāngai ana, me ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga a Google. Ka taea hoki e koe te here i ngā tautuhi pānui i ngā tautuhinga pūrere, te momotu i te whatunga, te whakamutu rānei i te whakamahi me te tango i te taupānga.
