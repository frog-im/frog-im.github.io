# International Data Transfer Notice

Effective date: 2026-06-12
Last updated: 2026-06-15

Lucky Pick Box does not send game entries, game results, or selected images to a developer-operated server. When ads or consent features are used, Google and related processors may process certain information outside your country or region.

1. Recipients and countries

Recipients may include Google LLC, Google affiliates, and processors used for advertising, consent management, security, measurement, and service operation. Processing may occur in the United States and other countries where Google or its processors maintain infrastructure.

2. Information that may be transferred

Information may include advertising identifiers, app instance identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and network information, app version, operating system, language, ad impressions, clicks, interactions, diagnostics, and ad consent or privacy settings. Game entries, participant labels, game results, and the selected image itself are not provided by the developer to Google advertising services.

3. Timing, method, and purpose

Transfer may occur through encrypted internet communications when the app requests ads, updates consent status, or opens privacy options. Purposes include ad delivery, non-personalized ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, consent management, measurement, service security, and legal compliance.

4. Retention and choices

Google and its processors retain information according to their privacy policies, contractual obligations, and applicable law. Where available, you may choose non-personalized ads, restricted data processing for applicable U.S. state privacy laws, or Google privacy options. You may also limit advertising identifiers in your device settings, disconnect from the network, or stop using and uninstall the app.
