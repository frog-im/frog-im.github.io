## 2. Ngā pārongo ka tukatukahia

### 2-1. Raraunga tuhipoka ka tāuru, ka waihanga tika te kaiwhakamahi

Hei tuku i ngā āhuatanga, ka taea e te taupānga te tukatuka i ēnei pārongo i runga i te pūrere.

- Ngā raruraru, ngā take, ngā mahi ka whai ake, me ngā tuhipoka whakatutuki i tuhia e te kaiwhakamahi
- Ngā kare ā-roto, te hiranga, ngā tūtohu, ngā kāwai me te tūnga oti
- Te wā waihanga, te wā whakatika me te tūnga tukatuka oti o te tuhipoka
- Ngā ingoa kōpaki, ngā tae me ngā kōrero kōpaki matua i waihangatia, i whakarerekētia rānei e te kaiwhakamahi
- Ngā hua tātaitanga ā-rohe mō ngā mata arotake, tatauranga, whakahaere macro me ngā tirohanga whakamuri

Ka tukatukahia ēnei pārongo i runga i te pūrere o te kaiwhakamahi hei tuku āhuatanga taupānga. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e tukuake ki tētahi tūmau a te kaiwhakawhanake.

### 2-2. Pārongo whakamotuhēhē me te haumarutanga

Hei tuku i te āhuatanga raka taupānga, ka taea e te taupānga te tukatuka i ēnei pārongo i roto i te rokiroki haumaru o te pūrere.

- Mēnā kua whakakāngia te raka taupānga
- Hash PIN me te salt
- Mēnā kua whakakāngia te whakamotuhēhē koiora
- Ngā kōrero tautuhinga pērā i ngā wā whakahou e pā ana ki te raka

Kāore te PIN taketake e rokirokitia, e tukuna rānei ki te kaiwhakawhanake. Ka mahia te whakamotuhēhē koiora e te pūnaha whakahaere; kāore te taupānga e kohikohi pikitia matimati, pikitia kanohi, tauira koiora rānei, ā, ka tirotiro noa mēnā i angitu te whakamotuhēhē.

### 2-3. Pūrua ā-rohe, whakahoki me te tiri

Ina kaweake, whakahoki, tiri tika rānei te kaiwhakamahi i ngā raraunga, ka taea e te taupānga te tukatuka i ēnei pārongo.

- Kōnae pūrua JSON kei roto ngā tuhipoka me ngā kōpaki
- Ingoa kōnae pūrua, wā waihanga me te hua whakahoki
- Kōnae atahanga ka waihangatia ina tiria ngā atahanga arotake
- Ara kōnae i kōwhiria e te kaiwhakamahi, ngā hua rānei o te kaitīpako tuhinga a te pūnaha whakahaere

Ka waihangatia ngā kōnae pūrua me ngā atahanga arotake ina kaweake, ina tiri tika rānei te kaiwhakamahi. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e whiwhi i ēnei kōnae i runga i tētahi tūmau a te kaiwhakawhanake.

### 2-4. Pārongo ka tukatukahia aunoatia pea i te pānuitanga me te whakaaetanga

I runga i ngā tūāpapa tautokona, ka taea e te taupānga te whakamahi i Google AdMob me UMP. Ka taea e Google me ngā kaiwhakarato hangarau pānuitanga te tukatuka i ēnei pārongo.

- Ngā tautohu pānuitanga, ngā tautohu instance taupānga, ngā tautohu e pā ana ki te pūrere rānei
- Wāhitau IP me te wāhi tata i matapaetia mai i te IP me ngā raraunga ōrite
- Tauira pūrere, pūnaha whakahaere, putanga taupānga, reo me ngā kōrero whatunga
- Tono pānuitanga, whakaaturanga, pāwhiri, taunekeneke me ngā kōrero tātaritanga
- Tūnga whakaaetanga pānuitanga me ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga ā-rohe

Ka tukatukahia pea ēnei pārongo mō te tuku pānuitanga, pānuitanga kāore i whakawhaiaroa, here auautanga, ine, ārai tūkino, whakahaere whakaaetanga, haumarutanga me te whakatutuki i ngā kawenga ture. Kua whakatinanatia te taupānga kia tono pānuitanga anake ina taea ngā tono pānuitanga i muri i te tukanga UMP.
