# Cross-Border Transfer Notice (AdMob-based)

This app (“**TimeBack**”) uses **Google AdMob** to provide in-app advertising and measure ad performance. In this process, personal information or information that may be considered personal information may be transferred outside the user's country.

---

## A. Recipient and Contact

- **Recipient:** Google LLC and its affiliates (AdMob service providers)
- **Role:** Ad delivery, advertising and measurement functions, related log processing, consent-state handling
- **Website:** `admob.google.com`
- **Privacy contact:** Google Privacy Help Center or other channels provided by Google

> Note: Google may use information collected from partner apps and sites, such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and app activity, for advertising and measurement purposes.

---

## B. Destination Countries

- The United States and other countries/regions where Google or its affiliates maintain data-processing facilities

---

## C. Timing and Method

- **Timing:** When the app runs and when ad requests, impressions, clicks, measurement, consent processing, or related functions occur
- **Method:** Transfer through encrypted network communication (HTTPS/TLS)
- **Safeguards:** Processing under relevant international data-transfer frameworks and contractual safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where applicable

---

## D. Purpose and Transferred Data

- **Purpose:** Ad delivery, personalization-state handling, performance measurement, analytics, service quality and stability improvement, fraud prevention, and legal compliance
- **Example data:** Advertising identifiers (AAID/IDFA), app version/settings, basic device/network information, cookie-like identifiers, ad interaction and error/crash signals, approximate location at city/country level, consent state, etc.

The actual information collected and used is subject to Google's partner data-use documentation and policies.

---

## E. Retention

- Information is retained for the period necessary to achieve the purposes above or for the period required by applicable law, then deleted or de-identified.
- Specific retention periods and deletion standards follow Google's privacy policy and advertising service policies.

---

## F. Right to Refuse or Withdraw Consent

- **In-app path:** Users can change advertising choices in the Privacy Options screen.
- In regions such as the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, users may select or reset personalized or non-personalized advertising choices through the UMP consent screen.
- Refusing or withdrawing consent may limit some personalized advertising features, but core app features remain available.
