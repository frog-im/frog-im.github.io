## A. Kaiwhiwhi me te Whakapā
- **Kaiwhiwhi:** Google LLC me ōna hononga (kaiwhakarato ratonga mō AdMob)
- **Ratonga/Tūranga:** tuku pānuitanga, āhuatanga pānuitanga/ine, tukatuka rārangi taki (log) e hāngai ana
- **Paetukutuku:** `admob.google.com`
- **Pātai tūmataiti:** Google Privacy Help Center, me ētahi atu ara whakapā tūmataiti e whakaratohia ana e Google
- **Tuhipoka:** I runga i iOS, ka ārahi ngā kaupapa here o te tūāpapa me te āhua o te whakaaetanga a te kaiwhakamahi i te whakamahinga o te tohu pānuitanga (IDFA).

> Tohutoro: Ka taea e Google te whakamahi i ngā mōhiohio ka kohia mai i ngā taupānga/pae hoa (ngā tohu pūrere, IP, mahi taupānga, me ērā atu) mō te pānuitanga me te ine.

## B. Ngā Whenua Haere
- Te Hononga-o-Amerika me ētahi atu whenua/rohe kei reira a Google, ōna hononga rānei, e āhei ana ki te tukatuka raraunga (hei tauira, i Uropi, i Āhia rānei).

## C. Te Wā me te Tikanga Whakawhiti
- **Wā:** ina rere te taupānga, ā, ina puta ngā tono pānui/whakaaturanga (impressions)/pāwhiri/ine
- **Tikanga:** ka tukuna mā runga whatunga me te whakamunatanga i te wā whakawhiti (HTTPS/TLS)
- **Ngā whakamarumaru:** ka tukatukahia i raro i ngā anga whakawhiti o te ao e hāngai ana, me ngā whakamarumaru kirimana (hei tauira, ngā whakatau ōritetanga, Standard Contractual Clauses)

---

## D. Kaupapa me ngā Momo Raraunga
- **Kaupapa:** tuku pānui, tūnga whakaritenga-ā-tangata (personalization), ine mahi, tātari (analytics), whakapai ake i te kounga me te pūmautanga o te ratonga
- **Ngā tauira raraunga:** ngā tohu pānuitanga (AAID/IDFA), putanga/tautuhinga taupānga, mōhiohio pūrere/whatunga taketake, ngā tohu rite-kuki (cookie-like), rārangi whakamahi (tae atu ki te pāhekoheko pānui me ngā tohu crash/hapa), tauwāhi whānui (taumata tāone/whenua).
  Ko ngā raraunga motuhake ka kohia ka whakahaerehia e ngā tuhinga a Google mō te whakamahinga raraunga a ngā hoa.

## E. Te Roanga Pupuri
- Ka pupuritia mō te wā e tika ana mō ngā kaupapa kei runga ake nei, mō te wā rānei e hiahiatia ana e te ture e hāngai ana; kātahi ka mukua, ka whakarerekētia rānei kia kore e tautohua (de-identified).

---

## F. Te Tika ki te Whakakore, ki te Tango Whakaaetanga rānei
- **Ara i roto i te taupānga:** i raro i **Privacy Options**, ka taea te kōwhiri i ngā pānui kore-whakaritenga (NPA).
  I ngā rohe pērā i EEA/UK/CH, ka taea te kōwhiri me te tautuhi anō i te whakaaetanga mā tētahi mata whakahaere whakaaetanga (CMP/UMP).
- Mēnā ka whakakore, ka tango rānei i te whakaaetanga, tērā pea ka iti iho ētahi āhuatanga whakarite-ā-tangata. Ka noho tonu ngā mahi matua o te taupānga.
