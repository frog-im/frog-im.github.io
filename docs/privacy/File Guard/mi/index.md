## 1. Whānuitanga me te kaupapa

He taputapu haumarutanga a FileGuard e āhei ai koe ki te pupuri i ngā kōnae kua tīpakohia, ngā pāpāho kua hopukina, ngā tuhipoka me te kuputuhi papatopenga ki roto i ngā pātaka kua whakamuna i runga i tō pūrere, ā, ki te waihanga i ngā kōnae tārua kua whakamuna.

Kāore te taupānga e tono rēhitatanga, takiuru rānei. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e whakahaere tūmau e whiwhi ana i ō kōnae pātaka, ihirangi kōnae, kupuhipa, ingoa pātaka, hītori ngohe rānei. Heoi, i runga i Android, ka whakamahi pea te taupānga i Google AdMob me Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) mō te pānuitanga me ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga.

## 2. Ngā mōhiohio e tukatukahia ana

### 2.1 Raraunga pātaka ka tīpakohia, ka waihangatia rānei e koe

Ka tukatuka pea te taupānga i ēnei mōhiohio i runga i tō pūrere:

- Ngā whakaahua, ataata, tuhinga me ētahi atu kōnae ka tīpakohia e koe mā te kaitīpako kōnae o te pūnaha
- Ngā whakaahua, ataata rānei ka hopukina mā te taupānga, tae atu ki te oro ka hopukina me tētahi ataata
- Kuputuhi papatopenga ka tiakina ā-ringa e koe, ka kitea rānei ina whakahohea e koe te whakamaru papatopenga aunoa
- Ingoa kōnae, momo MIME, rahi, wā waihanga, tapanga, me ngā mōhiohio e pā ana ki te arokite
- Ingoa me te tautuhi pātaka, whakarōpūtanga haumarutanga, tūnga kōnae taketake, me te tohutoro kōnae taketake
- Ngā mōkihi kaweake, whakawhiti, tārua rānei kua whakamuna ka waihangatia e koe

Ka tukatukahia ēnei mōhiohio i runga i tō pūrere hei whakarato i ngā āhuatanga o te taupānga. Kāore te kaiwhakawhanake e tukuake i aua mōhiohio ki tētahi tūmau e whakahaerehia ana e te kaiwhakawhanake.

### 2.2 Mōhiohio whakamotuhēhē me te haumarutanga

Ka tukatuka pea te taupānga i ēnei mōhiohio i roto i te rokiroki haumaru i runga i tō pūrere:

- Ngā salt, ngā tautuhinga whakaputa kī, me ngā paihere kī kua whakamuna e whakamahia ana hei whakaputa, hei tiaki rānei i ngā kī whakamuna
- Ngā taunaki urunga pātaka me ngā kī ā-rohe kua tiakina e tētahi kī herea ki te pūrere mō te urunga koiora
- Ngā manakohanga haumarutanga pēnei i te whakamaru whakaahua mata, te whakawātea papatopenga, te whakamotuhēhē anō ā-koiora me te whakamaru aunoa

Kāore tō kupuhipa kuputuhi-noa e tukuna ki te kaiwhakawhanake. Ka mahia te whakamotuhēhē koiora e te pūnaha whakahaere. Kāore te taupānga e kohikohi whakaahua matimati, kanohi rānei, tauira koiora rānei; ka whiwhi noa iho i te hua whakamotuhēhē.

### 2.3 Mōhiohio ngohe ā-rohe me te tārua

Ka rokiroki ā-rohe pea te taupānga i:

- Te momo, te whakaahuatanga, te wā me ngā tautuhi tūemi e pā ana ki ngā takahanga whakamaru, raka, muku, muku taketake, tārua me te whakahoki
- Tae atu ki te 500 tāurunga rangitaki ngohe
- Te ūnga tārua, te wā tārua whakamutunga, te kaute tūemi, me te tūnga angitū, rahua rānei
- Mēnā kua whakahohea te tārua aunoa me te kupuhipa e hiahiatia ana hei whakahou i taua tārua
- Ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga pānuitanga, te kōwhiringa tukatuka raraunga herea, me tētahi kaute tūemi kua tiakina e whakamahia ana mō te auautanga pānuitanga

Ka rokirokihia ngā tautuhinga tairongo, tae atu ki te tūnga tārua me te kupuhipa tārua aunoa, ki roto i tētahi rokiroki ā-rohe kua whakamunatia e te kī pūrere. Māu te kawenga ki te pupuri haumaru i tō kupuhipa tārua.

### 2.4 Mōhiohio e tukatukahia ana i te wā pānuitanga me te whakaaetanga

I runga i te tūāpapa pānuitanga e tautokona ana, arā ko Android i tēnei wā, ka whakamahi pea te taupānga i Google AdMob me UMP. Ka tukatuka pea a Google me ngā kaiwhakarato hangarau pānuitanga i:

- Ngā tautuhi e pā ana ki te pānuitanga, te tauira taupānga, te pūrere rānei
- Wāhitau IP me te tauwāhi tata ka whakaarohia mai i ngā mōhiohio pērā i te wāhitau IP
- Tauira pūrere, pūnaha whakahaere, putanga taupānga, reo me ngā mōhiohio whatunga
- Ngā tono pānuitanga, ngā putanga, ngā pāwhiri, ngā taunekeneke me ngā tātaritanga
- Tūnga whakaaetanga me ngā kōwhiringa tūmataitinga ā-rohe

Ka whakamahia pea ēnei mōhiohio mō te tuku pānuitanga, pānuitanga kāore i whakawhaiarotia, te aukati auautanga, te ine, te aukati tinihanga, te whakahaere whakaaetanga, te haumarutanga me te ū ki te ture. Kua whakatinanahia te taupānga kia tīmata te Google Mobile Ads SDK anake i muri i te tohu a UMP ka taea te tono pānuitanga.

Tirohia te [Kaupapahere Tūmataitinga a Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) me ngā [mōhiohio hangarau pānuitanga a Google](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 3. Ngā kaupapa o te tukatuka

Ka tukatuka a FileGuard i ngā mōhiohio hei:

- Rokiroki me te whakaatu i te ihirangi kua tīpakohia, kua hopukina rānei ki ngā pātaka kua whakamuna
- Whakahaere i te urunga ki te pātaka mā te raka, kupuhipa me te whakamotuhēhē koiora
- Kawemai, kaweake, nuku, muku me te whai i te tūnga kōnae taketake o te ihirangi
- Waihanga me te whakahoki i ngā tārua kua whakamuna ki tētahi wāhi ka tīpakohia e koe
- Pupuri i ngā manakohanga haumarutanga me te whakawātea i ngā kōnae rangitahi kua wetemunatia me te ihirangi papatopenga
- Whakaatu i te ngohe haumarutanga ā-rohe me te tūnga hapa
- Tuku pānuitanga Android, whakahaere i te auautanga pānuitanga me te whakarato kōwhiringa tūmataitinga
- Ārai i te whakamahi hē, tiaki i te ratonga me te ū ki ngā herenga ā-ture

## 7. Whakaaetanga pūnaha

Ka whakamahi pea te taupānga i ēnei whakaaetanga, āheinga pūnaha rānei ina whakamahia e koe te āhuatanga e hāngai ana:

- **Kōnae me ngā whakaahua:** Kawemai anake i te ihirangi ka tīpakohia e koe
- **Kāmera:** Hopu whakaahua, ataata rānei mō te pātaka
- **Hopuoro:** Whakauru oro ina hopu ataata
- **Koiora:** Whakaū i te urunga pātaka, ngā huringa tautuhinga tairongo rānei
- **Ipurangi me te tūnga whatunga i Android:** Tono pānuitanga AdMob me ngā mōhiohio whakaaetanga UMP

Mā te whakakore i tētahi whakaaetanga ka monokia pea te āhuatanga e hāngai ana anake. Ina whakamahia te kaitīpako kōnae o te pūnaha, ko te tikanga ka herea te urunga ki ngā tūemi ka tīpakohia e koe.

## 8. Ngā mahi haumarutanga me ngā herenga

Ka whakamahi te whakatinanatanga o nāianei i ngā mahi pēnei i:

- Whakamunatanga AES-256-GCM mō te ihirangi pātaka me ngā taupū pātaka
- Whakaputa kī PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 mō ngā kī e hāngai ana ki te kupuhipa
- Tiaki Android Keystore, StrongBox rānei mō ngā kī ā-rohe i runga i ngā pūrere Android e tautokona ana
- Whakamunatanga kī pūrere mō ngā tautuhinga tairongo me te hītori ngohe
- Raka pātaka me te horoi kōnae rangitahi me te papatopenga i runga i te whakapau kaha tika ina haere te taupānga ki muri
- Whakamaru whakaahua mata kōwhiringa me te whakamotuhēhē anō ā-koiora
- Ngā mōkihi tārua kawe kua whakamunatia ki te kupuhipa

Kāore he tikanga haumarutanga e whakakore i ngā mōrea katoa. Ka puta pea ngā mōhiohio i te tahae pūrere, malware, whakaraeraetanga pūnaha whakahaere, kupuhipa ngoikore, kōnae ka tiria e koe, raruraru haumarutanga rānei i tētahi kaiwhakarato rokiroki waho.

Mā te whakatuwhera, te kaweake rānei i te ihirangi kua wetemunatia ki tētahi atu taupānga ka waihanga pea i tētahi kape motuhake. Ka herea pea te muku kōnae taketake me te horoi kōnae rangitahi, papatopenga rānei e te pūnaha whakahaere; nō reira me manatoko koe i te tangohanga o te ihirangi tairongo.

Ka whakapā mai koe ki a mātou mō ngā mōhiohio i tukuna tika e koe ki te kaiwhakawhanake, pērā i tētahi īmēra pātai. Ina hāngai, ka tuku pea te ture ā-rohe i ngā motika uru, whakatika, muku, here, tango whakaaetanga, me te tuku amuamu ki tētahi mana tirotiro.
